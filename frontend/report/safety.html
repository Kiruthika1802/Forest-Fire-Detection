Safety Tips

1. Stay informed about local fire warnings and weather conditions.

2. Have an evacuation plan in case of a fire outbreak.

3. Keep emergency contacts and fire department numbers handy.

4. Practice fire drills with your family and coworkers.

5. Keep a fire extinguisher in your home and vehicle.

6. Follow safety guidelines when using fire pits or camping stoves.
