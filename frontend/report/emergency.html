Emergency Actions

1. If trapped, stay low to avoid smoke inhalation.

2. Find a water source or dig a small hole to avoid the heat.

3. Alert authorities and seek help immediately.

4. Use a flashlight or bright clothing to signal for help.

5. Stay calm and conserve energy while waiting for rescue.

6. Follow instructions from emergency responders and medical personnel.
