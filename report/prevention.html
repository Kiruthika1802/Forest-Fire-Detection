Prevention Tips

1. Avoid lighting open fires in dry forest areas.

2. Dispose of cigarettes properly, never throw them on the ground.

3. Clear dry leaves and debris that may ignite easily.

4. Follow local fire bans and regulations.

5. Store flammable liquids properly and away from heat sources.

6. Educate others about wildfire prevention and safe practices.
